# About me: story bank

## Career summary
Senior product manager with 11 years in B2B software, the last 6 in logistics. I lead a team of 4 PMs at Quillmoor Freight (fictional), a freight platform of about 600 people, where I own carrier onboarding and payments. Before that I was a PM at a payroll startup and started out as a business analyst at a freight brokerage, so I've sat on the operations side of the products I build.

## Stories

### Story: carrier onboarding from 9 days to 2
- Situation: new trucking carriers took 9 days on average to get approved, and about 30% gave up before their first load.
- What I did: sat with the onboarding team for a week, found that 60% of the delay was waiting on insurance documents, and shipped automatic insurance checks with two providers instead of manual review.
- Result: onboarding dropped to 2 days and drop-off fell from 30% to 11% in one quarter.
- Lesson: the slow step is usually a handoff between people, not the software.
- Good for questions about: delivery, using data, customer empathy

### Story: killing the quick-pay feature I pitched
- Situation: I championed same-day payment for carriers. Six months in, only 4% of carriers used it and it cost more to run than it earned.
- What I did: brought the numbers to my VP myself before the quarterly review, recommended shutting it down, and moved the two engineers to the onboarding work.
- Result: we shut it down in 3 weeks with no customer complaints; the onboarding work above came out of that freed-up team.
- Lesson: I now set a kill metric before launch, written down, for every bet.
- Good for questions about: failure, ownership, prioritization

### Story: disagreeing with sales about custom contracts
- Situation: sales wanted custom pricing logic for each of our 5 largest shippers. Engineering estimated a quarter of work for each one.
- What I did: pulled 18 months of contract data, showed that 4 of the 5 needs fit into three pricing rules, and proposed building those three rules instead.
- Result: sales agreed after a pilot with one shipper; we shipped the rules in 7 weeks and covered 4 of the 5 accounts.
- Lesson: bring the data to the person who disagrees before the meeting, not in it.
- Good for questions about: conflict, influence, stakeholder management

### Story: hiring my first PM [private: interview only]
- Situation: my first hire as a manager left after 5 months.
- What I did: asked for an honest exit conversation, learned I'd hired for skills and never discussed how much ambiguity the role had. Rewrote the interview loop with a written case on a messy, real problem.
- Result: the next 3 hires are all still on the team two years later.
- Lesson: describe the worst week of the job in the interview.
- Good for questions about: leadership, hiring, learning from mistakes
