# Interview rubric

Added on top of the default rubric for product leadership roles.

| Dimension | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Customer evidence | Names a real user conversation, observation, or data point behind the decision |
| Trade-offs named | Says what was given up and why, not just what was built |
| Team vs. me | Clear on what the team did, what I decided, and how I helped others do their best work |
