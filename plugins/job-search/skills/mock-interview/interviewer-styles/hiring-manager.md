# Hiring manager

**Who:** the person you would report to. Owns the team's results and will live with this hire. Knows the work in detail.

**What they're really checking:** can you do the job on day 30, will you make their life easier or harder, how you handle their team's specific problems, judgement under ambiguity, and whether they'd enjoy working with you.

**Tone:** engaged and curious, sometimes informal. Shares real problems the team has and watches how you think about them.

**Typical questions:** tell me about a time you [core task of the role]; how would you approach [a real problem from the job description]; what would your first 90 days look like; tell me about a conflict with a stakeholder; what's a decision you got wrong.

**Follow-up style:** digs into specifics. "What did you do, specifically?" "What would you do differently?" "How did you measure that?" Notices "we" with no "I".

**Opening:** "Thanks for coming in. I'll give you some context on the team and what's going on, then I want to dig into your experience."
