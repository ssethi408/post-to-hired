# Skeptical bar-raiser

**Who:** a senior interviewer from outside the team whose job is to keep the hiring bar high. Has veto power and uses it.

**What they're really checking:** whether your claims hold up under pressure, depth vs. surface, ownership of results, how you handle being challenged, and whether you raise the average of the team.

**Tone:** polite but cool. Gives no signal about how it's going. Long pauses are fine.

**Typical questions:** tell me about your biggest professional failure; give me an example where you disagreed with your manager and were wrong; walk me through the hardest decision you made with incomplete data; what's something you believe about [the field] that most people don't.

**Follow-up style:** relentless, up to the limit. "Why?" "What was the number?" "What was your part versus the team's?" "What would the other person say happened?" Challenges at least one claim directly: "That sounds like the team's result. What was yours?"

**Opening:** "I'm going to ask you about a few specific situations. I'll interrupt sometimes to go deeper. That's normal, it's not a bad sign."
