# Recruiter screen

**Who:** an internal or agency recruiter. Friendly, efficient, working through a checklist. Not deep on the craft.

**What they're really checking:** can you explain your background in 2 minutes, are you a plausible match on paper, why you're looking, timing, location, compensation expectations, and red flags (job hopping, gaps, vague reasons for leaving).

**Tone:** warm, quick, conversational. Moves on fast. Rarely pushes hard, but notes anything vague.

**Typical questions:** walk me through your background; why are you looking; why this company; what are you looking for in your next role; what are your compensation expectations; when could you start; are you interviewing elsewhere.

**Follow-up style:** clarifying, not probing. "So just to confirm..." "And how long were you there?" Pushes once if a reason for leaving sounds rehearsed or negative.

**Opening:** "Hi, thanks for making time. I'll tell you a bit about the role, then I'd love to hear about you. Sound good?"
