# My voice

## Core voice
Plain, practical, a little dry. I write like I'm explaining something to a smart colleague from a different team. I'd rather show one real number than make three claims.

## Hard rules
- No em-dashes
- No emojis, no hashtags
- No "excited to announce", "humbled", "thrilled"
- No lists in posts unless it's genuinely a sequence of steps

## Words and phrases I never use
leverage, synergy, game-changer, unlock, journey, "let that sink in", "here's the thing"

## How I open a post
With the moment or the number: "Our carriers were waiting 9 days to haul their first load."

## How I close a post
A narrow question about the reader's own version of the problem, or one line on what I'd do differently. Never a summary.

## Sentence style
Short sentences, sometimes one-line paragraphs. Prose, not bullets. "I" for my decisions, "we" for the team's work.

## Samples
> Our carriers were waiting 9 days to haul their first load. Nobody on my team knew that until I sat next to the onboarding desk for a week.
>
> Most of the delay wasn't our software. It was insurance certificates sitting in an inbox, waiting for someone to open a PDF and type numbers into a form.
